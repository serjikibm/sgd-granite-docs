What's new in Granite models

Draft content

This page is draft content for review. Entries are summarized from the model cards on Hugging Face.

New Granite models and updates, newest first. Announcements are published on the IBM Research blog and as articles on the ibm-granite organization page on Hugging Face. Each model card has the full details.

November 2026​

LLMON preview models​

November 12, 2026 · Language (preview)

LLMON is a new markup language that labels each part of a prompt, so the model can tell your instructions apart from the documents, data, and tool results it should only read. The Granite 4.1 3B and 8B LLMON preview models are trained to follow only the instructions they're pointed at, which makes them more resistant to prompt injection. These are experimental models, for exploration and evaluation rather than production use. See LLMON.

Granite 4.1 3B LLMON preview · Granite 4.1 8B LLMON preview · Announcement

October 2026​

Granite Switch 4.1.1​

October 27, 2026 · Switch

Granite Switch 4.1.1 updates the 8B and 30B Switch models with new weights for the decision adapter. Like the 4.1 release, a single endpoint serves chat, decisions, safety checks, and speech-to-text. This release shows how Switch models can be updated one adapter at a time, without replacing the whole model.

Granite Switch 4.1 8B · Granite Switch 4.1 30B · Announcement

Granite Speech 5.0 TurboCTC goes multilingual​

October 21, 2026 · Speech

Granite Speech 5.0 470M TurboCTC, built for high-throughput batch transcription, now supports seven languages: English, French, German, Spanish, Portuguese, Italian, and Japanese. It also adds native punctuation and capitalization, and improves English accuracy. A variant under a non-commercial license is also available.

Granite Speech 5.0 470M TurboCTC · Non-commercial variant

Granite Speech 5.0 2B​

October 20, 2026 · Speech

Granite Speech 5.0 2B is a compact speech-language model for multilingual speech recognition and bidirectional speech translation in English, French, German, Spanish, Portuguese, and Japanese. Compared to Granite Speech 4.1 2B, it has the same parameter count, with higher English transcription accuracy and better robustness to noise, reverberation, and distant microphones.

Granite Speech 5.0 2B · Granite Speech collection

Granite Switch 4.1​

October 14, 2026 · Switch

Granite Switch is a new family of modular models. Each Switch model combines a Granite 4.1 language model, task-specific adapters, and a speech encoder in one checkpoint, so a single endpoint can serve chat, decisions, safety checks, and speech-to-text. Adapters are selected per request. See Adapters and composition.

Granite Switch collection

August 2026​

Granite 4.2 language models​

August 25, 2026 · Language

Granite 4.2 adds built-in reasoning to the language models, in 3B, 8B, and 30B sizes. The models reason step by step inside <think>…</think> before answering, which improves math, coding, multi-step logic, and tool calling. You can switch between full thinking, non-thinking, and low-effort modes for each request. Each model is also published in GGUF, fp8, mxfp4, nvfp4, and MLX formats.

Granite 4.2 collection · Get started with Granite 4.2 3B

April 2026​

Granite 4.1 release​

April 29, 2026 · Language, Vision, Embedding

The Granite 4.1 release introduced new language models in 3B, 8B, and 30B sizes, Granite Vision 4.1 4B for document and image understanding, and the multilingual R2 embedding models in 97M and 311M sizes.

Granite 4.1 collection · Granite Vision 4.1 4B · Granite Embedding collection · Announcement

Granite Guardian 4.1​

April 2026 · Guardian

Granite Guardian 4.1 8B detects risks in prompts, responses, and agentic workflows, including harmful content, hallucinations, and jailbreak attempts. Use it alongside a language model as a guardrail. See Safety and security.

Granite Guardian 4.1 8B · Granite Guardian collection
