Granite is a family of open models from IBM, published under permissive licenses on Hugging Face. This section helps you get oriented, run your first model, understand concepts that apply across all models, and verify what you download. For the latest releases across all families, see What's new.

Model families​

Each family links to its collection on Hugging Face, where every model card has full details, benchmarks, and usage instructions.

FamilyWhat it is forHugging Face
LanguageGeneral purpose instruction-following, text-generation, tool-calling, and agentic capabilities.Collection
SwitchModular LLMs that compose a Granite base model, embedded LoRA adapters, and a speech encoder into one checkpoint, so a single endpoint serves chat, decisions, safety checks, and speech-to-text.Collection
VisionUnderstanding images and documents.Collection
SpeechSpeech recognition, bidirectional speech translation, enterprise transcription.Collection
EmbeddingSemantic search, retrieval, and RAG pipelines.Collection
GuardianSafety and risk-detection models for identifying harmful content, hallucinations, toxicity, and policy violations in LLM inputs and outputs.Collection
DoclingSpecialist document-parsing model for converting complex PDFs and structured documents into machine-readable formats.Collection
Time SeriesForecasting, anomaly detection, and classification of enterprise time series data.Collection
LibrariesCollections of task-specific LoRA adapters (RAG, Guardian, core capabilities) that extend and customize Granite base models without full retraining.Collection
