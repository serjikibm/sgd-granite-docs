NeurIPS 2026 is almost here. The conference runs from December 6 to December 12, 2026, with the main event in Sydney, Australia, and satellite locations in Atlanta and Paris. IBM is returning as a Platinum sponsor and is presenting work at all three locations.

{/*truncate*/}

## Find us at the booth

Visit **IBM booth #73** from Monday, December 7, through Thursday, December 10. The booth has demos across IBM Research, including foundation models, trustworthy AI, natural language processing and understanding, knowledge and reasoning, AI automation, human-centered AI, and federated learning.

## Papers and sessions

IBM's accepted papers, talks, and workshops will be listed on the [IBM at NeurIPS 2026](https://research.ibm.com/events/neurips-2026) page as the schedule is published.

## Talk to us about Granite

If you're attending, these are good conversation starters:

- **LLMON**, the markup language that helps models tell instructions from data. See [LLMON: teaching Granite to tell instructions from data](./2026-11-12-llmon-preview.mdx).
- **Granite.build**, for running LLM pipelines from a single `build.yaml`. See [Granite.build: one file from model download to evaluation](./2026-11-19-granite-build.mdx).
- **Your use case.** Tell us what you're building with Granite and what you'd like to see next.

## Can't make it?

Follow along on the [IBM at NeurIPS 2026](https://research.ibm.com/events/neurips-2026) page, and join the conversation in [Granite GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/orgs/ibm-granite/discussions).
